### 🌱 About Me

- 🔭 AI Developer at **ICRISAT** (International Crops Research Institute for the Semi-Arid Tropics), working under the **Mission Mausam** initiative
- 🏗️ Primary architect of two production platforms:
  - **[ISAT](https://isat.icrisat.org)** — an LLM/RAG-powered agricultural advisory platform delivering block-level crop, weather, and contingency recommendations across Maharashtra
  - **[BIKAS](http://65.0.242.164/bikas_in/)** — a national climate analytics platform built on Apache Druid, aggregating block-to-national-level data with a RAG layer for natural-language querying
- 🎓 M.Tech in Computer Science and Automation from **IISc Bangalore**
- 🔐 Academic background in cryptography research — distributed random beacons, decentralized bulletin boards, and Vickrey auction security proofs
- 🛠️ I care about deterministic, auditable decision engines as much as the LLM layer on top of them
- 💬 Ask me about RAG pipelines, agricultural decision systems, or scaling Django + Druid in production
